Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

The Government of Purvanchal provides monthly financial assistance of Rs. 250 under the State Social Security Pension Scheme to all eligible senior citizens aged 60 and above residing across Sonari, Rajapara, Dhemaji Pathar, Borgaon, Namti, and Khelua blocks.

How to Complete Your Online Application

1. Mobile Verification
Verify your 10-digit mobile number with a one-time password (OTP).
2. Personal & Bank Details
Enter applicant name, DOB, village, block, and bank IFSC details.
3. Age Proof Upload
Upload voter ID, birth certificate, or Aadhaar (JPG/PDF up to 5 MB).
4. Digital Acknowledgment
Submit statutory declaration and instantly download your signed receipt.
Need Help? For offline assistance or document scanning, visit your nearest Block Development Office or Citizen Seva Kendra.
