About the Old Age Pension Scheme

The Old Age Pension Scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal, under the State Social Assistance Programme and National Social Assistance Scheme.

🎯 Statutory Eligibility

  • Permanent resident of Purvanchal.
  • Minimum age of 60 years or above.
  • Individual applicant (one application per citizen).

⚖️ Statutory Deemed Approval

Applications are processed strictly within 15 days as per the Purvanchal Right to Public Services Act. Applications not decided within this statutory period stand approved automatically.
